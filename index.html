Common questions

What kinds of cleaning jobs do you take?

All types. Homes, rentals, listings, buildings, offices and anything in between, big or small, one-time or recurring. If it needs cleaning, tell us about it and we'll plan the job around the property.

Do you take small jobs?

Yes. We handle small jobs ourselves and bring in a team for large homes, buildings and multi-unit projects.

Can you work with my listings or rental units regularly?

Yes. We work with realtors, property managers, landlords and short-term rental hosts on one-time projects and recurring schedules.

Do you do move-out cleaning for security deposits?

Yes. Move-in and move-out cleans cover the empty home top to bottom, including inside appliances, cabinets and drawers on request, so the place is ready for the walkthrough.

How is pricing set?

Each quote is based on the property's size, condition and what you want done. Send the form or call 857-753-5593 for a free quote.

Do you clean offices?

Yes. We clean offices and small businesses, including a recurring commercial client in Cambridge.

What areas do you serve?

We clean across Greater Boston, including Boston, Dorchester, Cambridge, Somerville, Brookline, Jamaica Plain, South Boston, Roxbury, Quincy, Milton, Newton, Medford, Malden, Everett and Revere. We often travel farther for larger jobs, so call if your town isn't listed.
