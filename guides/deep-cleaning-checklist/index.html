Deep cleaning checklist: 6 spots most cleanings skip

A regular clean keeps surfaces tidy. A deep clean gets the places that collect buildup over months. Start with these six.

The six spots

  • Cabinet fronts and handles
  • Baseboards and trim
  • Inside the oven
  • Light switches and door handles
  • Window tracks and sills
  • The range hood filter

Then the rest

  • Dust high first: fans, vents, tops of frames
  • Then counters and surfaces
  • Then glass and mirrors
  • Vacuum and mop last

Pro tip

Work top to bottom and dry to wet, so nothing lands on a surface you already cleaned.
