Holiday cleaning checklist: before and after guests

Guests notice the entry, the guest bathroom and the kitchen. Do the big jobs a week out, the touch-ups the day before, and plan the reset for after.

A week before

  • Inside the fridge, cleared to make room
  • Inside the oven, before the big roast
  • Guest room: fresh sheets, dusted surfaces, empty drawers
  • Baseboards, door frames and light switches
  • Windows and mirrors in the rooms guests use

The day before

  • Entry and front door, mat shaken out
  • Guest bathroom scrubbed, fresh towels and spare paper out
  • Kitchen counters cleared and wiped
  • Dining table and chairs wiped down
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped

After everyone leaves

  • Inside the fridge again, leftovers sorted
  • Stovetop, oven door and range hood
  • Floors under the table and chairs
  • Sheets and towels washed
  • Trash and recycling out

Pro tip

Book the deep clean for the week before and the reset for the day after. Holiday weeks fill up fast, so ask early.
