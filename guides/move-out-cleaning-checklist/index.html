Move-out cleaning checklist to get your deposit back

Landlords check the same places at almost every walkthrough. Clean these, room by room, and you'll hand back the keys with nothing to argue about.

Kitchen

  • Inside the fridge and freezer, shelves and drawers out
  • Inside the oven, racks and the door glass
  • Inside the microwave
  • Inside every cabinet and drawer
  • Stovetop, range hood and filter
  • Sink, faucet and backsplash

Bathrooms

  • Tub, tile and grout
  • Toilet, including the base and behind it
  • Sink, mirror and medicine cabinet
  • Exhaust fan cover

Every room

  • Closets and shelves wiped out
  • Baseboards, door frames and switches
  • Window sills and tracks
  • Spot-clean scuffs on walls
  • Floors vacuumed, then washed

Pro tip

Clean top to bottom, and do the floors last, after the last box is out. Take dated photos of each room when you're done.
