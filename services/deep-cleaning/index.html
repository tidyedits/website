What is a deep clean?

A deep clean goes past everyday surfaces into buildup and detail: baseboards, trim, grout, fixtures, vents, range hoods and the spots a regular clean skips. It is the best first visit before recurring service or a reset before guests. Tidy Edits deep cleans homes across Greater Boston and brings a team for large homes.

What's included

  • Baseboards, trim, door frames and light switches
  • Kitchen: cabinet fronts, backsplash, range hood and appliance exteriors
  • Bathrooms: grout lines, fixtures, soap scum and buildup
  • Dusting high and low, including vents and ceiling fans
  • Inside appliances, cabinets and drawers on request
  • Floors vacuumed and washed, edges included

Every clean is tailored to the property. Need something not listed? Ask. We do all types of cleaning jobs.

Who it's for

First cleans before recurring service, seasonal resets, homes before guests or holidays, and anyone who wants a proper fresh start.

Around Greater Boston

Older Boston housing has a lot of detail: radiators, wood trim, built-ins and tall windows. Those are exactly the places that collect dust, so we plan extra time for them.

Questions

How is a deep clean different from a regular clean?

A regular clean keeps a home in shape. A deep clean gets the buildup and the detailed spots first, so regular visits stay quick after.

How long does it take?

It depends on size and condition. We'll estimate it in your quote and bring a team for larger homes.
