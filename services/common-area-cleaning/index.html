What does common area cleaning cover?

Common area cleaning keeps the shared parts of a building presentable: entrances, lobbies, hallways, stairs, railings, mailbox areas, glass doors and laundry rooms. Tidy Edits cleans common areas for multi-family and multi-level residential buildings across Greater Boston, on a recurring schedule or as a one-time reset.

What's included

  • Hallways, landings and stairways vacuumed and mopped
  • Entrances, vestibules and lobbies
  • Railings, doors, handles and light switches wiped
  • Glass doors and mailbox areas
  • Trash and recycling areas tidied on request
  • Weekly, biweekly or monthly schedules

Every clean is tailored to the property. Need something not listed? Ask. We do all types of cleaning jobs.

Who it's for

Property managers, condo associations, landlords and developers.
