How does recurring house cleaning work?

You pick weekly, every two weeks or monthly, and the same detailed standard happens every visit: kitchens, bathrooms, dusting and floors, plus a rotating detail task like baseboards. Visits can be moved with notice. Tidy Edits runs recurring routes across Boston and nearby towns, and many clients start with one deep clean.

What's included

  • Kitchens: counters, sink, stovetop, appliance fronts
  • Bathrooms: toilet, tub or shower, sink, mirrors
  • Dusting reachable surfaces and furniture
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Beds made and trash out on request
  • A rotating detail task each visit, like baseboards or the inside of the microwave

Every clean is tailored to the property. Need something not listed? Ask. We do all types of cleaning jobs.

Who it's for

Busy households, families, people who work long hours, and anyone tired of spending weekends cleaning.

Questions

Do I need a deep clean first?

Often it helps. If the home hasn't had a professional clean in a while, a first deep clean makes every regular visit better.

Can I skip or move a visit?

Yes. Just give us notice and we'll move it.
