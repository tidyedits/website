What is a pre-sale listing clean?

A listing clean gets a home photo-ready and showing-ready: glass, fixtures, floors, kitchens and baths detailed, and the small things buyers notice, like switch plates and baseboards. Tidy Edits works with Greater Boston sellers, realtors and brokers, and has done full pre-sale cleans like a 5-bedroom home in Dorchester.

What's included

  • Windows, glass and mirrors streak-free for photos
  • Kitchens and baths detailed, fixtures shining
  • Baseboards, doors, switches and trim
  • Inside cabinets and appliances, because buyers open them
  • Vacant-home and post-renovation cleans
  • A final walk-through before photos

Every clean is tailored to the property. Need something not listed? Ask. We do all types of cleaning jobs.

Who it's for

Realtors, brokers, home sellers, developers and estate sales.
